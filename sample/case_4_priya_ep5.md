# Scenario: Priya (Episode 5)

**Prompt:** Priya writes up her notes after the dress rehearsal.

## Permitted Facts (Narrative Boundary)
- Kamala announces to the assembled company that the season will close with a revival of The Winter Boarders, with all four company members present.
- Tomas arranged Priya's hire directly by phone; Priya is Tomas's sister's daughter.Kamala is not consulted about this hiring.
- Kamala briskly cuts Wren's only scene in front of Dev and Priya
- Wren cries on the hamper in the costume store after having her scene cut, and Priya sits with her on the floor without saying much, telling no one about it afterwards.
- 14 years ago, Kamala forged her missing co-founder's signature on the roof grant application.
- Priya finds the carbon copy of the grant application in a box of programmes, realizes Kamala forged the signature, puts it back, and tells no one.
- Dev misses an entire day of rehearsal without offering an explanation, and Kamala chooses not to press him about it.
- Priya quietly rewrites the understudy plot so Wren covers Dev's role without clearing it with Kamala, and instructs Wren to start learning the part.
- Tomas tells Priya between two cars that he is selling the building; Priya agrees to maintain secrecy.
- The lighting desk fails halfway through the second act, forcing Kamala, Dev, Priya, and Wren to stay past midnight. Tomas is not present and is never told.
- Priya pays for the replacement lighting desk out of her own account and secretly files the paperwork as a hire charge.
- Kamala puts Wren on in Dev's part during the dress rehearsal rather than lose the run, and Wren performs extraordinarily for two and a half hours.
- Dev arrives an hour late as the dress rehearsal is ending, apologises to Priya in the car park, and assumes the run was cancelled. Priya does not correct him.

## Generated Scene
The last echoes of Wren’s performance fade into the quiet of the rehearsal space as Priya shuts the door, the cold night air biting her cheeks. She pulls out her tablet, the screen’s soft glow cutting through the dimness of the room. Dust motes swirl in the light, catching the scent of stale coffee and old stage makeup.

Her fingers move steadily across the keyboard, the sound of the keys a sharp counterpoint to the silence. She types: *Wren covers Dev role (2.5 hours, extraordinary). Understudy plot revised—no Kamala approval.* The cursor blinks, a small pulse in the stillness. She pauses, glancing at the clock: 1:47 AM. Outside, the city is asleep, but the weight of the night—the lighting desk failure, the unfiled costs—hangs heavy in the air.

She adds a final line without looking up: *Lighting desk: replaced. Cost covered by Priya. Paperwork filed privately.* Pressing send, she closes the tablet, the screen darkening against the night. The room holds its breath, the silence now a quiet testament to the quiet decisions she’s made.
