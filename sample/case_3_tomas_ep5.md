# Scenario: Tomas (Episode 5)

**Prompt:** Tomas walks through the empty auditorium the morning after signing the sale agreement.

## Permitted Facts (Narrative Boundary)
- Kamala announces to the assembled company that the season will close with a revival of The Winter Boarders, with all four company members present.
- Tomas tells Kamala the lease expires in 11 months and will not renew automatically.
- Tomas warns Kamala that the lease expires in eleven months and will not renew automatically, and Kamala tells no one about the conversation.
- Tomas arranged Priya's hire directly by phone; Priya is Tomas's sister's daughter.Kamala is not consulted about this hiring.
- Tomas has lunch with a property developer on Bellary Road and leaves with architectural drawings.
- Tomas tells Priya between two cars that he is selling the building; Priya agrees to maintain secrecy.
- Tomas describes the tenancy as 'under review' at the board meeting and quickly moves on.
- Tomas signs the conditional sale agreement for the building at the developer's office on Friday morning.

## Generated Scene
The cold seeps up from the auditorium’s floor tiles, sharp as a knife against Tomas’s soles. He walks past the empty stage, the dust motes caught in the late-morning sun slanting through the high windows. His footsteps echo too loudly in the silence, a sound that feels like a promise he can’t keep.  

He stops at the door handle, fingers pressing against the cool metal. The air smells of old wood and something dry—like forgotten promises. For a second, he imagines Kamala’s face in the mirror of the boardroom where he’d described the lease, the way she’d nodded without speaking. *Under review*, he’d said. Now the word hangs in the emptiness of the stage, heavy as the silence.  

He turns the handle. The door swings open onto the empty hallway, where the smell of the developer’s architectural drawings lingers faintly. Tomas doesn’t look back. He walks away, the building’s last secret already in his chest.
