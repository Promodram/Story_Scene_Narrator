# Scenario: Kamala (Episode 4)

**Prompt:** Kamala goes over what she saw in the car park.

## Permitted Facts (Narrative Boundary)
- Kamala announces to the assembled company that the season will close with a revival of The Winter Boarders, with all four company members present.
- Tomas tells Kamala the lease expires in 11 months and will not renew automatically.
- Tomas warns Kamala that the lease expires in eleven months and will not renew automatically, and Kamala tells no one about the conversation.
- Kamala briskly cuts Wren's only scene in front of Dev and Priya
- Kamala finds the 2019 arts council grant file and locks it into her desk drawer alone.
- 14 years ago, Kamala forged her missing co-founder's signature on the roof grant application.
- Dev misses an entire day of rehearsal without offering an explanation, and Kamala chooses not to press him about it.
- Kamala watches Tomas and Priya speaking in the car park from the fire escape; she hears nothing and concludes Tomas is going behind her back.
- Tomas describes the tenancy as 'under review' at the board meeting and quickly moves on.
- Kamala asks Dev to headline a closing-night fundraising gala; Dev agrees ambiguously.

## Generated Scene
Kamala’s fingers trace the rough concrete of the fire escape railing, the scent of wet asphalt and distant exhaust clinging to her clothes. Below, the parking lot is empty except for the slow roll of two tires on the pavement—a sound she’d heard twice that week. She’d watched Tomas and Priya from the edge of the building’s shadow, just beyond the chain-link fence.  

Tomas’s voice had been quiet, but she’d heard the way he’d paused when Priya mentioned the lease. *“Under review,”* he’d said at the board meeting, his words smooth but his eyes fixed on the floor. Now, she saw him turn toward Priya’s car, his shoulders tense, his hand resting on the door handle. She’d stood silent in the shadows, waiting for the next word. There was none. Only the silence between them, heavy as the weight of the lease’s expiration date—eleven months, and no renewal.  

She turned her head sharply, the movement sharp against the stillness. Her glasses slipped low on her nose. *He’s not talking to me.* The thought cut through her. She hadn’t told anyone about the conversation with Tomas. Not Dev. Not Priya. Not even herself, really. Now she knew what she’d seen: Tomas moving behind her back, just like he’d said he would.  

She stepped back from the railing, the cold metal of the fire escape pressing against her shoulder. Her watch told her it was 7:17 p.m. The last light from the streetlamps glinted on the edge of the parking lot. She didn’t look down at the file in her desk drawer—yet. Not tonight. She closed her eyes for a second, the city’s hum fading to the distant chime of a train. Then she turned and walked toward the building, the air thick with the memory of that silence.  

She paused at the door, her hand hovering over the handle. The drawer was locked. But she knew the cold metal would stay locked, too. For now.
